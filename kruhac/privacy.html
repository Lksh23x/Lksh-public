Privacy Policy

Kruhač — Last updated: May 2026

Kruhač ("the app") is a circuit-training timer for iOS developed by LKSH. This policy explains how the app handles your data.

Data Collection

The app collects no personal data. No information about you or your device is ever transmitted off your device.

Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores the following data locally, solely on your device:

  • Workout presets you create (name, station/round configuration, audio settings)
  • Session history (date, duration, participant count, intensity)
  • App preferences (theme, display settings)
  • Instant-session parameters saved between launches

This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to the developer or any third party. Deleting the app removes all stored data permanently.

Third-Party Services

The app contains an optional integration that opens the Spotify app via a deep link. Kruhač does not communicate with Spotify's servers directly — it only launches the Spotify app already installed on your device. Any data handling within Spotify is governed by Spotify's Privacy Policy.

Analytics & Advertising

The app contains no analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or tracking technologies of any kind.

Children's Privacy

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the revised version will be posted at this URL with an updated date at the top.

Contact
