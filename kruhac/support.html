Getting Started

How do I create a workout preset?

Open the Presets tab and tap the + button. Give it a name (3–16 characters), set the number of stations and rounds, configure durations and audio, then save. You can have up to 4 presets.

How do I start a session?

On the Home tab, select a preset or use the quick-start controls, set the participant count, and tap Start. The timer opens automatically.

What is the "Getting Ready" countdown?

Before the first workout phase begins, the app counts down using your Transition Warning duration. Use this time to get participants in position.

---

During a Workout

How do I pause or stop the timer?

Tap the screen during an active session to reveal the controls overlay. Use Pause to freeze the countdown and Stop to end the session early. Stopped sessions are not saved to history.

What does the flashing overlay mean?

When the countdown enters the transition warning window, the screen flashes to give participants a heads-up that the phase is about to end. The warning duration is configurable per preset.

What is inter-round rest?

After the last station of any non-final round, the rest period is automatically doubled. This gives participants extra recovery time before the next round begins.

Audio

Audio stopped working after I opened another app

Some apps (Mail, Spotify, phone calls) can interrupt the audio session. Return to Kruhač and it will reactivate automatically. If audio still does not play, force-quit and reopen the app.

Can I use Kruhač while music is playing?

Yes. Kruhač is configured to mix with background audio — beeps and go-signals play on top of whatever is playing without pausing or ducking it.

How do I change the beep sounds?

Open a preset in the Presets tab and change the Beep Style and Go Signal settings. Each preset can have its own audio configuration.

Data & Privacy

Where is my data stored?

All data (presets, session history, settings) is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

How do I delete all my data?

Delete the app from your device. All data is removed permanently with no server copy to worry about.

Is session history backed up?

Only if you have iCloud or iTunes device backup enabled — in that case your data is included in your device backup. That backup is controlled entirely by you and Apple.
